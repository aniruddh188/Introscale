Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

1. Overview

At Introscale, we are committed to delivering high-quality, performance-driven digital marketing and lead-qualification services. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines how our fees are structured and the exact terms under which you may request a refund or cancel your service agreement.

2. Fee Structure & Non-Refundable Costs

To ensure full transparency, our engagements are divided into three distinct cost categories:

  • Tech & Infrastructure Setup Fee (Non-Refundable): Any upfront onboarding, commitment, or setup fee ($250–$500 or as specified in your service agreement) is strictly non-refundable once work is initiated. This fee covers immediate hard third-party infrastructure expenses incurred on your behalf, including CRM sub-account provisioning, US carrier A2P 10DLC compliance and campaign registration, dedicated tracking phone numbers, domain/funnel setup, and telecommunication usage credits.
  • Third-Party Advertising Spend (Non-Refundable): All advertising spend is billed directly to your credit card by third-party advertising platforms (e.g., Meta Ads, Google Ads). Introscale does not collect, hold, or mark up your ad spend, and therefore cannot issue refunds for payments made to advertising networks.
  • Performance / Service Fee (Refund-Eligible): Our core management and lead-qualification service fee is covered by our performance guarantee, subject to the eligibility criteria in Section 3.

3. Refund Eligibility (Service Fee Only)

Refunds apply strictly to the Introscale Service Fee and are applicable only under the following circumstances:

  • Unmet Performance Guarantee: If we fail to deliver the agreed-upon number of qualified leads or booked appointments within the timeframe outlined in your signed Service Agreement.
  • Duplicate Billing: If a duplicate payment was processed due to a verifiable technical error.
  • Uninitiated Service: If you paid a deposit or service fee, but Introscale failed to initiate onboarding or account setup within 10 business days due to internal reasons on our part (in this specific instance, the Setup Fee will also be refunded).

4. Client Cooperation & Conditions That Void the Guarantee

Because campaign performance relies on timely execution, the performance refund guarantee is void if any of the following occur:

  • Ad Account or Card Failures: Your ad account is paused, restricted, or fails to spend the agreed-upon daily budget due to declined credit cards, billing limits, or client-side payment delays.
  • Onboarding Delays: Failure to provide required onboarding assets, business verification details (EIN/tax details required for US A2P 10DLC SMS registration), or ad account access within 5 business days of sign-up.
  • Unauthorized Campaign Edits: You or a third party manually pause, alter, or delete active ads, landing pages, or CRM automations built by Introscale during the guarantee period without written approval.
  • Lead Handling Failure: Failure to attend scheduled appointments or follow up with qualified leads/estimates delivered to your calendar or pipeline.

5. Refund Process & Cross-Border Transaction Fees

To request a refund under an eligible Service Agreement, please email info@introscale.com with your business name, campaign summary, and supporting details.

  • Verification & Timeline: Once your request is verified against campaign and CRM data, approved refunds will be processed within 7–14 business days.
  • Payment Rail & Method: Refunds are issued via the original payment method (e.g., Card, PayPal, or ACH Debit) or via an agreed electronic bank transfer/remittance method if the original payment was completed via direct wire/ACH push transfer.
  • Deduction of Processing & Forex Fees: Any approved refund will be calculated minus non-recoverable third-party payment processing fees, international wire/ACH fees, and currency conversion (forex) spreads incurred during both the initial collection and the outward refund transfer.

6. Cancellation Policy

  • Before Campaign Launch: If you choose to cancel after onboarding has begun but before ads go live, any pre-paid Service Fee is eligible for a refund (minus payment processing/forex fees); however, the Tech & Infrastructure Setup Fee remains non-refundable as software and telecom registrations are incurred immediately upon onboarding.
  • During the Guarantee Period: Cancellations requested mid-campaign (before the 30-day evaluation window ends) are not eligible for a refund, as the campaign is actively in execution.
  • Ongoing Subscriptions / Retainers: After the initial term, clients may cancel recurring monthly services by providing at least 14 days’ written notice prior to the next billing cycle via email to info@introscale.com. Cancellations submitted after a billing cycle renews will take effect for the following billing period.

7. Exceptions

No refunds will be issued once the guaranteed number of qualified leads or appointments stipulated in the Service Agreement has been achieved, or if CRM and call-tracking logs verify that the agreed-upon deliverables were met.
