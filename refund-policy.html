Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

1. Overview & Post-Paid Performance Model

At Visible Impact, we operate on a transparent, performance-first model where our core service fee is only collected after your initial campaign period or agreed-upon deliverables are completed. Because we assume the upfront performance risk and do not collect our management fee in advance, traditional post-service cash refunds do not apply. This policy outlines our billing structure, non-refundable setup costs, and cancellation terms.

2. Fee Structure & Non-Refundable Payments

Our client engagements are structured into three clear components:

  • Tech & Infrastructure Onboarding Fee (Non-Refundable): To initiate a campaign, we collect a one-time upfront onboarding and infrastructure fee (typically $250–$500, or as specified in your Service Agreement). Because these funds are immediately deployed to cover hard third-party setup costs—including CRM sub-account provisioning, US carrier A2P 10DLC SMS compliance registration, dedicated tracking numbers, AI voice/SMS setup, and funnel builds—this onboarding fee is strictly non-refundable once onboarding is initiated.
  • Post-Paid Service / Performance Fee (No Fee If Guarantee Unmet): Our core service fee is billed only after the agreed monthly cycle or performance milestone is achieved. If Visible Impact fails to deliver the guaranteed results outlined in your signed Service Agreement (subject to Section 4), you simply do not pay the Service Fee. Once results are delivered and a post-paid Service Fee invoice is settled by the client, that payment is final and non-refundable.
  • Third-Party Advertising Spend (Non-Refundable): All advertising budgets are paid directly by you to the advertising platforms (e.g., Meta Ads, Google Ads) using your own payment method. Visible Impact never collects or holds your ad spend and cannot issue refunds for platform ad charges.

3. Rare Exceptions Where a Refund Applies

Because all payments made to Visible Impact are either hard infrastructure onboarding fees or post-delivery settlements, refunds are only issued under two narrow circumstances:

  • Duplicate Billing Error: If you were accidentally charged twice for the same invoice due to a verifiable technical or payment processor error.
  • Uninitiated Onboarding: If you paid the upfront Onboarding Fee, but Visible Impact failed to initiate your account setup or onboarding within 10 business days due to internal reasons on our part.

In either of the eligible scenarios above, please email info@getvisibleimpact.com. Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days, minus any non-recoverable third-party payment processing or currency conversion (forex) fees.

4. Client Responsibilities & Performance Guarantee Conditions

Our commitment to waiving the post-paid Service Fee if results are not met requires active client cooperation. Your performance fee waiver is void—and the full agreed Service Fee becomes immediately billable and due—if campaign delivery is prevented by any of the following client-side actions:

  • Ad Account Payment Failures: Your ad account is paused, restricted, or unable to spend the agreed daily budget due to declined cards, billing thresholds, or funding delays.
  • Onboarding & Verification Delays: Failure to provide required business verification details (such as EIN/tax records required for US A2P 10DLC SMS registration) or ad account access within 5 business days.
  • Unauthorized Campaign Edits: Pausing, modifying, or deleting active ads, landing pages, or CRM qualification workflows built by Visible Impact without prior written approval.
  • Lead Neglect: Failure to show up to booked appointments or follow up with qualified leads delivered to your calendar or pipeline.

5. Cancellation Policy

  • Before or During the First 30 Days: If you choose to cancel your engagement after onboarding has begun or mid-way through the initial 30-day campaign window, the upfront Onboarding Fee remains non-refundable, as infrastructure and telecom registrations have already been incurred.
  • Ongoing Monthly Campaigns: Following the initial campaign period, clients may cancel future monthly cycles by providing at least 14 days’ written notice prior to the end of the active service period via email to info@visibleimpact.com. Any completed deliverables or active billing periods already in progress remain payable upon completion as outlined in your Service Agreement.
