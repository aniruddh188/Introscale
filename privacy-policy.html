Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Visible Impact ("we," "us," "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how and why we collect, store, use, and process your information when you use our digital marketing, CRM infrastructure, and automated lead-qualification services ("Services").

This includes when you:

  • Visit our website or any dedicated campaign landing page that links to this privacy notice.
  • Engage with us as a business client and purchase our marketing and lead-generation services.
  • Interact with our advertising campaigns (e.g., on social media or search engines), opt in to receive SMS or voice communications, or request an estimate/appointment with one of our clients.
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including strategy calls, sales, or customer support.

This Privacy Policy applies both to our business clients (e.g., pressure washing and exterior cleaning businesses, collectively "Clients") and to the individuals ("End-Customers" or "Leads") who submit inquiries through dedicated client campaigns managed by Visible Impact.

Reading this privacy notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at info@getvisibleimpact.com.

2. Definitions

Client: The exterior cleaning, pressure washing, or home service business entity that contracts Visible Impact to manage its digital marketing and lead-qualification infrastructure.

End-Customer (or Lead): An individual who expresses interest in a Client's services by submitting a form, calling, or texting a dedicated campaign number or landing page.

Personal Information: Any information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular individual or household.

Data Fiduciary / Controller vs. Data Processor: With respect to our own B2B Clients and website visitors, Visible Impact acts as the Data Fiduciary/Controller. With respect to End-Customer (Lead) inquiries captured on a dedicated campaign page built for a specific Client, Visible Impact acts as a Data Processor/Service Provider operating directly on behalf of that named Client.

3. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

We collect Personal Information from different sources depending on how you interact with us:

3.1. Information You (Our B2B Client) Provide to Us

When you book a strategy call, onboard as a Client, or communicate with us, we collect:

  • Contact, Identity & Business Compliance Data: Your first and last name, email address, phone number, business legal name, physical business address, website URL, and business tax identification (such as a US Employer Identification Number / EIN required strictly for US A2P 10DLC telecom registration).
  • Account & Integration Credentials: Login or administrative partner access details needed to configure your CRM sub-account, calendars, and third-party ad accounts.
  • Payment & Billing Information: Payment details required to settle your upfront onboarding fee and post-paid service invoices (processed securely via third-party gateways and banking partners such as Razorpay, ACH/Fedwire, or PayPal).

3.2. Information Collected from End-Customers (Leads)

When an End-Customer interacts with a campaign or landing page managed on behalf of a Client, we collect:

  • Contact Data: Name, email address, mobile/landline phone number, and property/service address.
  • Service Qualification & Call/SMS Logs: Details regarding the requested service (e.g., driveway washing, house washing, roof cleaning, square footage, timeframe), as well as transcripts and recordings of inbound/outbound calls and SMS conversations used to qualify the request and book appointments.

3.3. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website or landing pages, we automatically collect technical information, including IP address, browser type, device characteristics, operating system, referring URLs, and interaction data via cookies and pixels (such as the Meta Pixel or Google tag). Please see Section 12 for our Cookie Policy.

4. How and Why We Use Your Information

We process Personal Information strictly for legitimate, explicit business purposes:

  • To Provision & Deliver Our Services: Setting up CRM sub-accounts, registering US A2P 10DLC messaging campaigns with telecom carriers, managing ad campaigns, and processing invoices.
  • To Qualify Leads & Book Appointments (Including Automated SMS & AI Voice): Contacting End-Customers who submit inquiries via automated SMS workflows and AI-assisted voice calls to confirm project details, provide quote ranges, and schedule estimates directly onto our Client's calendar.
  • To Communicate with Clients: Sending onboarding updates, campaign performance reports, billing invoices, and support notifications.
  • For Analytics & Security: Monitoring campaign performance, optimizing landing page conversions, and preventing fraudulent activity.
  • To Comply with Legal & Telecom Obligations: Meeting tax, anti-fraud, RBI/export invoicing regulations, and US carrier compliance rules.

5. Our Legal Bases for Processing

We process Personal Information under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: Fulfilling our obligations under our Service Agreement with our Clients or responding to an End-Customer's service request.
  • Express Consent: Processing End-Customer phone numbers and emails submitted via opt-in forms for SMS and voice communications, as well as B2B marketing consent. Under India's DPDP Act and US TCPA regulations, you may withdraw consent at any time (for example, by replying STOP to any SMS).
  • Legitimate Interests & Legal Obligations: Maintaining platform security, improving services, and complying with applicable laws.

6. Disclosure and Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your Personal Information to data brokers or third parties. Information is only disclosed in the following limited ways:

  • Direct Delivery to the Designated Service Provider (Client): When an End-Customer submits a quote request for a specific Client's services, that information is collected directly on behalf of that single Client so they can fulfill the estimate or appointment. Leads are never resold or shared across multiple unrelated businesses.
  • Infrastructure & Sub-Processors: We utilize trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as CRM hosting, cloud telecommunication carriers, AI processing APIs, and payment processors) strictly to operate the Services under confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: Where required by law, subpoena, or regulatory authority.

Strict Mobile & SMS Opt-In Protection: Notwithstanding anything else in this policy, no mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data will ever be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

7. SMS, Automated Voice & A2P 10DLC Mobile Privacy Terms

In compliance with US telecom carrier regulations (A2P 10DLC) and the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA):

  • No Third-Party Sharing of Mobile Data: Mobile information and text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Opt-In Consent: By providing your phone number on our website or any campaign form and opting in, you consent to receive transactional, informational, or appointment-related text messages (SMS/MMS) and phone calls (which may utilize automated dialing technology or AI voice assistants) from Visible Impact or the specific business from which you requested a quote.
  • Opt-Out (STOP) Instructions: You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Simply text "STOP" to the number from which you received the message. After you send the SMS message "STOP" to us, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from that number.
  • Help (HELP) Instructions: If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program, you can reply with the keyword "HELP" for more assistance, or reach out directly to info@getvisibleimpact.com.
  • Carrier Disclaimer & Rates: Message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. Message frequency varies based on your interaction and appointment status. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. International Transfer of Personal Information

Visible Impact is headquartered in India. If you access our Services or campaign forms from the United States or Canada, your Personal Information may be accessed, stored, or processed by our team in India and on cloud servers located in the United States. We implement strict access controls and contractual safeguards to ensure your information remains protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws (including CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, and India's DPDP Act).

9. Data Security, Integrity, and Retention

We implement technical and organizational security measures (including encrypted connections, role-based access, and secure payment gateways) to safeguard the Personal Information we process. We retain Personal Information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, maintain accurate tax and compliance records, or resolve disputes. When no ongoing legitimate business need exists, we securely delete or anonymize the data.

10. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Request access to and obtain a copy of your Personal Information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Information.
  • Request deletion/erasure of your Personal Information.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing or automated communications at any time (e.g., replying STOP to SMS or clicking Unsubscribe in emails).

To exercise any of these rights, please email info@getvisibleimpact.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
